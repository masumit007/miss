# Fundamental Analysis & Classic Frameworks
## 1. Piotroski F-Score (0 to 9)
Evaluates Profitability (Positive ROA, CFO, ΔROA, CFO > Net Income), Leverage/Liquidity (ΔDebt, ΔCurrent Ratio, No Dilution), and Operating Efficiency (ΔGross Margin, ΔAsset Turnover).

## 2. Classic Investment Frameworks
- **Warren Buffett Quality**: Durable Moat, 10-Yr Avg ROE > 18%, conservative debt, intrinsic value bands.
- **CANSLIM**: Current earnings, Annual growth, New products/catalysts, Supply/Demand, Leader, Institutional sponsorship, Market direction.
- **Benjamin Graham Deep Value**: Graham Number $\sqrt{22.5 \times \text{EPS} \times \text{BVPS}}$, P/E × P/B < 22.5.
- **Peter Lynch Growth Engine**: Fast Grower, Stalwart, Cyclical, Slow Grower, Turnaround, PEG multiple.
