# Technical Indicator Formulas & Reference
## Supported Indicators
- **RSI (14)**: Relative Strength Index with 70 overbought and 30 oversold benchmarks.
- **MACD (12, 26, 9)**: Fast EMA 12, Slow EMA 26, Signal 9, Histogram, zero-line transitions.
- **Moving Averages**: SMA 20, 50, 100, 200 and EMA 20, 50, 100, 200. Golden Cross (50 > 200 SMA) and Death Cross (50 < 200 SMA).
- **Bollinger Bands (20, 2)**: 20 SMA with ±2 standard deviations; Bollinger Squeeze detection.
- **ADX (14)**: Average Directional Index with +DI and -DI for trend strength.
- **Support & Resistance**: Pivot swing points and Fibonacci retracements (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%).
- **Candlestick Patterns**: Doji, Hammer, Hanging Man, Bullish/Bearish Engulfing, Morning/Evening Star, Marubozu.
