# Data Sources & Lineage Hierarchy
MISS NEPSE strictly enforces transparency into data provenance and live scraping pipelines.

## Hierarchy of Sources
1. **Live Continuous Scraping & Market Feed Tier**:
   - [HamroShare](https://hamroshare.com.np): Live ticker prices, market capitalization, 52-week highs/lows, EPS, P/E ratio, Book Value, Graham numbers, turnover, volume, and IPO pipeline.
   - [Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE)](https://www.nepalstock.com/): Official index levels, sectoral indices, market breadth, circuit limits, and trading halts.
2. **Primary Regulatory Tier**:
   - Securities Board of Nepal (SEBON) regulatory filings and approvals
   - NEPSE Corporate Disclosures & AGM/Dividend announcements
   - Audited Quarterly & Annual Reports from listed entities
3. **Authoritative Macroeconomic Tier**:
   - Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) Monetary Policy, Repo Rate, CPI Inflation, Forex Reserves, and Remittance Bulletins
4. **Secondary Verified News Tier**:
   - Bizmandu, Sharesansar, OnlineKhabar Business, Nepali Paisa
5. **AI & Quantitative Synthesis**:
   - Proprietary Multi-Factor Scoring (0-100), Graham Fair Value, Technical Indicator calculations (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR), and sentiment classifications clearly labeled as analytical outputs.
