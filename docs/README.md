# MISS NEPSE — Mini Intelligent Stock System
**AI-Powered Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) Market Intelligence & Stock Research**
*Founder & Developer: Built by Sumit (Sumit Kumar Mahato)*

MISS NEPSE is an institutional-grade, AI-driven stock research, quantitative screening, and intelligence platform designed specifically for the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE). It continuously scrapes and ingests live market data from [HamroShare](https://hamroshare.com.np) and [NEPSE](https://www.nepalstock.com/).

## Core System Positioning
- **Primary Scope**: Quantitative Research, Screening, Fundamental & Technical Analysis, Graham Fair Valuation, Smart Money Flow Tracking, News Sentiment, IPO Analytics, and Educational Decision-Support.
- **Market Timezone & Schedule**: Nepal Time (NPT, UTC+5:45), Trading Days: Sunday – Thursday (11:00 AM – 3:00 PM NPT).
- **Currency**: Nepalese Rupee (**NPR** / **Rs.** / **रू**), denominated in Arb (1 Billion NPR) and Crores (10 Million NPR).
- **Strict Compliance Boundary**: No trade execution, no broker integration, and zero guaranteed profit claims.

## Key Modules
- **MISS NEPSE Markets**: Live Indices (NEPSE Index, Sensitive Index, Float Index, Banking, Hydropower, Microfinance, Manufacturing, Insurance, Hotels, Investment), Market Breadth (Advances/Declines/52W Highs), Total Turnover, Volume, and NRB Macro Stance.
- **MISS Stocks**: Global live search across 504+ NEPSE listed companies, tickers, ISINs, and sectors with 13-tab deep analysis per security.
- **MISS Screeners**: 15+ quantitative presets (Hydro Power Breakouts, Commercial Bank Undervalued, Graham Fair Value, Microfinance Momentum, Piotroski 8-9) + Custom Screener + AI Natural Language Screener.
- **MISS Technicals**: 14+ indicators (RSI 14, MACD 12/26/9, SMAs/EMAs, Bollinger Bands, ADX, Candlesticks, S/R, Fibonacci Retracements).
- **MISS Fundamentals**: Profitability, Solvency, Cash Flows, and Classic Frameworks (Piotroski F-Score 0-9, CANSLIM, Buffett Moat, Graham Deep Value Formula, Peter Lynch Fair Value).
- **MISS Smart Money**: Institutional trends, Promoter holding/pledge, Bulk/Block deal tracking, Positional delivery absorption.
- **MISS AI & Picks**: Tool-grounded AI Chatbot, Multi-Factor Scoring (0-100), automated PDF-ready Research Reports, and continuous AI Watchlists.
- **MISS News & IPO**: AI sentiment-tagged news stream, event extraction, and live IPO tracking with subscription multiples and allotment status.
