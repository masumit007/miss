# Multi-Factor Scoring Methodology (0–100)
## Subfactor Weight Calibration
- **Fundamentals (25%)**: ROE, ROCE, Piotroski score, cash conversion.
- **Technicals (20%)**: Price vs 50/200 SMAs, RSI momentum, MACD alignment.
- **Growth & Quality (15%)**: 3-Yr Revenue and Net Profit CAGRs.
- **Valuation (15%)**: PEG ratio, P/E vs historical median.
- **Smart Money (10%)**: Institutional FII/DII additions and low promoter pledging.
- **Risk Profile (5%)**: Beta volatility and leverage safety.
- **News Sentiment (5%)**: Ratio of positive corporate announcements.
- **Macro & Sector (5%)**: Sector momentum vs NIFTY 50 benchmark.
